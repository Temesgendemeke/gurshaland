# Gurshaland: Ethiopian Culinary Platform

**Gurshaland** is a modern web platform dedicated to discovering, sharing, and preserving authentic Ethiopian recipes and culinary traditions. It serves as a digital hub for Ethiopian food culture.

## Core Mission

To empower the community to contribute family recipes, explore diverse dishes, and celebrate Ethiopia's rich food heritage through a modern digital experience.

## Key Features

### 1. Recipe Discovery & Sharing

- **Recipe Collection**: A vast library of authentic Ethiopian recipes.
- **User Contributions**: Users can submit their own recipes, complete with ingredients, cooking instructions, images, and cultural context.
- **Search & Filter**: Powerful search capabilities to find recipes by category (e.g., Stews, Breads, Vegan), tags, or keywords.
- **Favorites**: Users can save recipes to their personal collection for easy access.

### 2. Meal Planning

- **Personalized Plans**: Users can generate meal plans tailored to their preferences (e.g., Vegan, Vegetarian, Standard).
- **Nutritional Goals**: Supports goals like fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance.
- **AI-Powered Generation**: Utilizes AI to create weekly or daily meal plans that respect Ethiopian dietary customs (like fasting seasons) and nutritional needs.

### 3. Restaurant Finder

- **Explore Dining**: A feature to discover Ethiopian restaurants (implied by file structure).
- **Restaurant Pages**: dedicated pages for restaurants to showcase their offerings.

### 4. AI Assistant

- **Culinary Expert**: An AI chatbot capable of answering questions about Ethiopian cuisine, suggesting recipes, and helping with meal planning.
- **Context Aware**: The AI understands the nuances of Ethiopian ingredients (like Teff, Berbere, Shiro) and cooking techniques.

### 5. Community & Culture

- **Cultural Notes**: Recipes often include historical and cultural significance, educating users about the traditions behind the food.
- **User Profiles**: Manage personal contributions and saved items.

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: Next.js 14, React, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui
- **Backend**: Supabase (Auth & Database)
- **AI**: Google Gemini (integration for chat and content generation)

## Terminology

- **Gursha**: An Amharic term meaning "mouthful," often referring to the act of feeding someone else as a gesture of love and respect.
- **Injera**: A sour fermented flatbread with a slightly spongy texture, traditionally made of teff flour.
- **Wot**: Ethiopian stew or curry, often spicy.
- **Berbere**: A key Ethiopian spice blend.
